# Your Studio

Hi Nat! Studio is your private home base for everyone who inquires: who they are, where they are on the way to
booking you, what they paid, their signed contract, and the dates on your calendar. It lives at
**curatedbynat.com/studio**. Only you and Matt can open it. It's built for your phone, and it works on a laptop too.

Every inquiry from your website lands here by itself, so you never have to copy anything over.

---

## 1. Signing in

1. Go to **curatedbynat.com/studio**.
2. Type **hello@curatedbynat.com** and tap **Send me a code**.
3. Open that inbox, find the 6-digit code, and type it in.

That's it: no password to remember. You stay signed in for about a month on the same phone. To sign out, tap
**Settings**, then **Sign out**.

## 2. Put it on your home screen

On your iPhone, open Studio in Safari, tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**. Now it opens like an app.

## 3. Finding your way around

The bar at the bottom (or the menu on the left on a computer) has five places:

- **Home**: what needs you today.
- **Pipeline**: everyone, grouped by where they are on the way to booking.
- **Calendar**: events, consultations and reminders.
- **Clients**: everyone in one searchable list.
- **Settings**: your packages, messages and calendar link.

The round **+** button is always there. Tap it to add a lead, add something to your calendar, or find someone to
add a note to. **Search** at the top finds anyone by name, email, phone number or Instagram.

## 4. Your daily routine

Open **Home**. It shows, in this order:

- **Needs follow-up**: people who have been quiet for 5 days, or whose follow-up date has come. (You can change the
  5 in Settings.)
- **New inquiries**: people nobody has replied to yet.
- **This week**: events, consultations, deliveries and reminders in the next 7 days.
- **Balances due**: money you're still waiting on, due in the next two weeks or already late.

Tap someone to open them. When you've been in touch, tap **Called**, **Texted**, **Emailed**, **DM'd** or **Met**
(you can type what was said first). That's all: it starts the quiet-days count again and moves a brand new person
to **Contacted**.

If you'd rather be nudged on a certain day, pick a date under **Follow up on** and tap **Save**. The person shows on
Home that day.

## 5. Adding someone

Tap **+**, then **A lead**. Only a first name is needed. Use it for people who messaged you on Instagram or
TikTok, were referred, or inquired somewhere else. (Website inquiries are added for you.) If you add someone whose
email is already in Studio, it tells you and links to them instead of making a duplicate.

## 6. Working with a person

Open anyone and you'll find:

- **Buttons at the top**: **Call**, **Text**, **Email** and **Instagram** open your own apps.
- **Log contact**: write down what you did. **Or just add a note** for something that isn't a conversation.
- **Reply**: pick a saved message (like "Packages and availability"), tap **Preview**, and you'll see it already
  filled in with their name, date and details. **Open in Mail** opens it in your email app to read over and send.
  **Copy message** lets you paste it anywhere, like Instagram DMs. Studio never sends anything by itself. Tap
  **I sent it** afterwards to note it on their timeline.
- **Where they are**: move them forward through **New, Contacted, Consultation, Packages sent, Booked, Event done,
  Delivered**. **Mark as lost** asks why (so you can see patterns later), and **Reopen this lead** brings them back
  if they change their mind.
- **Calendar**, **Files**, **Timeline** (everything that's happened, with who did it and when), and **Details**
  with **Edit details**. **From the website** shows exactly what they typed in the inquiry form.

## 7. Booking someone

When they say yes, open them and tap **Book** followed by their name (or choose **Booked** under Stage, which opens the same form).

1. Pick their **package** (or **Something custom**), the **extras**, and a **travel fee** if it applies.
2. Leave **Total** blank to have it added up for you, or type your own.
3. Add the **retainer** and when the **balance is due** (it suggests a month before the event).
4. Tap **Book this client**.

Their booking card then shows the total, what you've received and the balance. Each time they pay, fill in **Amount
received**, the date, how they paid (Venmo, Zelle, cash, check, card), and tap **Record payment**. The status moves
from **Unpaid** to **Retainer paid** to **Paid in full** on its own. If you change a package price in Settings
later, bookings you've already made keep their original price.

## 8. Saving the signed contract

You get contracts signed outside Studio, then save the signed PDF here. On the person's page, find **Files**, tap
**Choose File** (on your phone you can pick from your photos, take a picture of a page, or choose a PDF from
Files), choose **Signed contract**, and tap **Save file**. Until one is saved, their booking shows a reminder.
Studio keeps PDFs, JPGs, PNGs and HEIC photos up to 25 MB.

## 9. Your calendar

Booked events and the day-after delivery show up by themselves, and change if you change the person's date. Add
your own things with **Add to calendar**: a consultation, a reminder, or something personal. Tap a day to see it.

**See it on your phone's own Calendar app:** go to **Settings**, then **Calendar on your phone**, and tap **Add to
iPhone Calendar**. Tap **Subscribe**. New things appear there within about an hour, and your phone can remind you
before events. It only goes one way, from Studio to your phone. It shows first names, what kind of thing it is, the
time and the city, and nothing private. If you ever share the link by accident, tap **Make a new link**.

## 10. Making it yours (Settings)

- **Follow-ups and lost leads**: how many quiet days before someone is flagged, and your list of reasons.
- **Reply templates**: four starter messages are there, marked **Draft**. Read them and change them until they
  sound like you, then untick **Still a draft**. Blanks like `{first_name}` fill in by themselves.
- **Packages** and **Extras**: change names, prices and what's included. **Hide** one you don't offer any more.

## 11. Clients and spreadsheets

**Clients** lists everyone in every stage. Search, sort, or show one stage. **Export to a spreadsheet** downloads
the list for Excel or Google Sheets (names, dates, packages and money).

## 12. If you delete something

Nothing disappears right away. **Settings, Recently deleted** keeps deleted people, files and calendar items for 30
days, and **Restore** brings them back. After 30 days you can choose **Delete forever**, except for signed contracts
and for anyone who has a booking or files, which Studio keeps.

## 13. If something looks off

- A page says **Not found** after you signed in: tell Matt. It means the sign-in settings need a quick fix.
- You can't get the sign-in code: check spam, wait a minute, and ask for a new one.
- Something you changed doesn't look right: open the person and look at their **Timeline**. It shows what changed.

Questions? Ask Matt. And tell him what would make Studio easier for you; it's yours.
