# Editing your website

Hi Nat! This guide shows you how to change the words and photos on curatedbynat.com yourself. You only
need a web browser. There is nothing to install, and you can't break the design: the layout stays the
same no matter what you type or which photos you choose.

---

## 1. Signing in

1. Go to **curatedbynat.com/keystatic**.
2. Click **Sign in** and use your Keystatic Cloud email and password (the account set up with your
   personal email).
3. You'll land on the editor's home screen. Bookmark this page.

It works on a computer, tablet, or phone, but a computer is easiest for photos.

---

## 2. Finding your way around

The list on the left is grouped the way the website is:

| In the editor | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Pages** > Home page, About page, Services page, Inquire page, Page not found (404) | The words and photos on each page |
| **Services and photos** > The four services | Your four services: the cards on the Home page and the full sections on the Services page |
| **Services and photos** > Instagram photos (Home page) | The "More of my work, over on Instagram" section: its words and the four photos you picked by hand |
| **Services and photos** > Instagram feed (automatic photos) | Whether the Instagram section and the "Inquire now" slideshow show your latest Instagram posts by themselves |
| **Inquiry form** | Every question, choice, and message on the inquiry form |
| **Every page** > Menu, footer and social links | The menu, the footer, your Instagram and TikTok links, and the photos that slide behind "Inquire now" |

Each page is split into sections that match the website from top to bottom, for example
"Top of the page", "How it started", "Closing banner". Click a section to open it.

## 3. Changing words

1. Open the page or section you want to change.
2. Click into a box and type. Every box has a short note underneath that says where it shows up.
3. Click **Save** (top right).

**When does it show up?** About 2 to 3 minutes after you click Save. Refresh the website to see it.
Each save updates the live site, so it's fine to save often.

Good to know:

- **Small headings in capital letters** (like "HI, I'M NAT"): type them normally ("Hi, I'm Nat"). The
  website turns them into capitals for you.
- **Lists** (paragraphs, photos, choices): each item has its own row. Use **Add** to add one, the bin icon
  at the right of an item to remove it, and drag the handle on the left to reorder.
- **Required boxes**: if you leave one empty, Save won't work and the box turns red with a note. Fill it
  in and save again. Inside a list, an item with something missing is labeled with a ⚠ warning (for
  example "⚠ Needs a photo description"); open it and fill in what it says.
- **Client quotes** (Kind words, Client quote): these are your clients' exact words. Paste them exactly
  as they sent them, without quotation marks (the website adds a pretty one).
- **"Page title" and "Page description"** (under "Search and sharing" on each page): what Google shows
  for that page. Keep titles under 60 characters and descriptions under 155.

---

## 4. Swapping a photo

1. Find the photo you want to change. Each photo box says where it appears and its shape, for example
   "tall 4:5 crop" or "wide 3:2 crop".
2. Click **Choose file** under the current photo and pick your new one. It replaces the old photo.
3. **Update the "Photo description (alt text)"** box under it. Describe the new photo in one sentence,
   for example "Bride laughing with her bridesmaids on a porch". This is read aloud to visitors who use
   screen readers and helps Google, so it's required.
4. Check **"Keep in view"**. When a photo is cropped to fit its space, this decides which part stays
   visible. For people, "Upper third (faces)" usually works best.
5. Click **Save**.

Photo tips:

- **Use JPG or PNG photos.** Photos straight from your phone are fine; the website resizes and
  compresses them and removes location details from them. On a Mac, if a photo is in HEIC format
  (the Photos app's default), export it as a JPEG first: in Photos choose **File > Export > Export 1 Photo**
  and set **Photo Kind** to **JPEG**.
- **Portrait photos** (taller than wide) suit almost every spot on the site.
- **The big photo on the Home page** has your name written over it. Pick one without a big bright white
  area in the middle, or the text becomes hard to read. It has two "Keep in view" settings, one for
  computers and one for phones, because phones show a narrower slice of the photo.
- **The slideshow behind "Inquire now"** (bottom of Home and Services) lives in
  **Every page > Menu, footer and social links > Closing banner slideshow photos**. Use 4 to 12 photos.
  These are decorative, so they don't need a description.

## 5. Editing your four services

Open **Services and photos > The four services**. Each service has:

| Box | Where it shows |
|---|---|
| Service name | Card title on Home, and the small heading above the section on Services |
| Card text on the Home page | The one-line blurb on the Home page card |
| Card photo on the Home page | The wide photo on the Home page card |
| Headline, Description on the Services page | The section on the Services page |
| What you receive | The list in that section (add, remove, or drag to reorder items) |
| Button text | The button in that section; it always goes to the inquiry form |
| Photo on the Services page | The tall photo in that section |

There are always exactly four services. You can drag them to change their order on both pages.

**Leave "Link name" as it is.** Buttons on the Home page use it to jump to the right service.

## 6. Your Instagram photos

Two places on the website can show your newest Instagram posts by themselves: the four photos in
"More of my work, over on Instagram" on Home, and the slideshow behind "Inquire now". Once your
Instagram is connected, a new post appears on the website within about half a day. Nothing for you to do.

**To turn it on or off:** open **Services and photos > Instagram feed (automatic photos)**.

- **Instagram section on Home** and **Slideshow behind "Inquire now"**: choose "My latest Instagram posts"
  or "The photos I picked". You can choose differently for each.
- **How many posts in the slideshow**: 4 to 12.
- **Include Reel and video covers**: Reels show their cover picture. Turn this off to show only photo posts.
- **Hide a post**: not everything belongs on the website. Open the post on Instagram, tap the three dots,
  choose Copy link, then click **Add** here and paste it. It disappears at the next update.

**Your hand-picked photos are the backup.** If Instagram can't be reached, or there aren't enough posts yet,
the website quietly shows the photos you picked instead. To change those: open
**Services and photos > Instagram photos (Home page)** (the four for the Home section) or
**Every page > Menu, footer and social links > Closing banner slideshow photos** (the slideshow), and swap
them as in section 4.

Each photo in the Home section opens that post on Instagram. The photo descriptions for screen readers come
from the first sentence of your caption. Posts whose caption mentions prices are described generically.

Your Instagram and TikTok links and handles are in **Every page > Menu, footer and social links >
Social links**. Your Instagram handle is also typed into three messages in **Inquiry form** (the "Yay,
it's in!" message, the message shown if an inquiry can't be sent, and the note for browsers with
JavaScript turned off), so if your handle ever changes, update those too.

## 7. The inquiry form

Open **Inquiry form**. You can reword any question, the hint inside a box, the messages people see when
they miss something, the Send button, and the "Yay, it's in!" message after sending.

- **Choices** in the "Who's inquiring?", photographer, and "How did you find me?" dropdowns can be added,
  removed, and reworded.
- **"What are you celebrating?" and "Is your event in Georgia?"** always keep the same choices, because
  the form reacts to them (for example, the End date box appears for bachelorette weekends and
  celebrations). You can reword those choices but not add or remove them.

**Where inquiries go:** every inquiry is emailed to **hello@curatedbynat.com**. Just hit Reply to answer
the person directly. A backup copy of every inquiry is also saved, so nothing is lost if an email goes
astray.

---

## 8. If something doesn't look right

- **Your change isn't showing after 5 minutes:** refresh the page (on a phone, close and reopen the tab).
  If it still isn't there, ask for help: the update log shows what happened. If a photo you added is
  missing from the page but your other changes appeared, the photo is in a format the site can't read
  (usually HEIC). Upload it again as a JPG.
- **You changed something by mistake:** type it back the way it was and save. Every past version is kept,
  so anything can be restored. Just ask.
- **Save isn't working:** something required is empty. Look for a red box with a note under it, and open
  any list item labeled with a ⚠ warning (for example "⚠ Empty" or "⚠ Needs a photo").
